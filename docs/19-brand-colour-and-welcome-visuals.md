# 19 — Brand colour and welcome visuals
2026-11-18 | Owner direction U08 | Palette proposal pending visual approval; no assets or app code produced.

## Owner requirements
Lime is the main accent for the entire application, including splash logo pulse, edge glow, loading bar and primary actions. The background is a dark green with a translucent black overlay that gives it a greyish, earthy brown-green/olive character, expressed as a gradient. Welcome explains benefits in plain English and includes a beautiful original picture. This supersedes the electric-blue and plain-black proposals in docs 08, 17 and 18.

## Proposed colour tokens
| Token | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| AccentLime | #C6FF45 | Primary buttons, progress, selected controls |
| AccentGlow | #A8EF35 | Soft halo, animation highlight |
| BackgroundDeep | #111710 | Dark near-black green |
| BackgroundOlive | #292C1B | Muted earthy brown-green midpoint |
| BackgroundShade | #181D16 | Charcoal green gradient end |
| Surface | #252B20 | Cards and sheets |
| TextPrimary | #F4F5EC | Main text on dark surfaces |
| TextSecondary | #B9C1AF | Supporting text |
| OnAccent | #111710 | Text on lime buttons |

Suggested background: diagonal gradient #292C1B → #181D16 → #111710; restrained translucent black overlay on underlying green/olive artwork where needed. Keep lime bright but limited to emphasis; avoid neon background or strong brown/orange cast. Exact hex values, gradient direction and intensity are proposals, not owner-approved measurements. Check contrast, accessibility, and system bar appearance on representative displays. Do not use lime alone to convey money/status.

## Splash and welcome
Splash: centered logo, lime edge pulse, slim lime progress bar with visible numeric percentage, bottom attribution exactly "powered by LX Obsidian Labs", then spin/shrink/fade and screen exit. Percentage describes completion of actual defined readiness milestones (0, 25, 50, 75, 100 for four completed milestones), not elapsed time or a promised ETA. Progress only advances on completed work and 100% only when destination is ready; no artificial delay. Error displays recovery instead of fictitious completion. Reduced-motion mode remains available.

Welcome: same dark olive gradient for visual continuity; original hero artwork in upper area, practical benefit-led headline and supporting text, prominent lime Get started button, unobtrusive Restore backup action. Suggested art direction: warm editorial illustration of a small-business owner using a phone, understated lime highlights, subtle money/receipt/customer visual motifs. Keep UI text separate from illustration for accessibility/localization. No generated illustration yet; final character style, composition and logo pending approval.

Proposed copy: "Know where your money goes." / "See what you earn, what you spend and who still owes you." / "Free to use. Works offline." Explain additional benefits in onboarding. No technical jargon or profit claims.
