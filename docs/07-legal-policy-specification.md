# 07 — Legal policy specification
2026-11-18 | requirements, not final legal advice or published terms
## Website and in-app terms
Public HTML (no login, no editable shared doc), effective date, version history, identifiable developer and verified support/privacy contact. Mirror versioned text in app for offline reading. Onboarding explains terms/privacy and records acceptance of terms separately from privacy/ad choices. Do not make ad consent acceptance a condition for core offline functionality.
## Terms of use / EULA sections
Developer identity and contact; eligibility and license; free core and ad-supported model; business record ownership; lawful use and user responsibility for accurate input, tax status, backups, sharing and retention; scope of financial tools and limitations; availability/updates/support; IP and third-party licenses; termination and local deletion; limitation of liability subject to non-waivable consumer rights; South African law and complaints/disputes. Review CPA/ECTA applicability before publishing. No claim of SARS endorsement, professional advice or formal accounting compliance.
## Privacy policy
Map categories: business/customer data local; attachments/backup/export user controlled; ad SDK data transmitted (IP, interactions, diagnostics, identifiers subject to settings/version); support correspondence if user contacts developer; website logs/cookies according to actual site configuration. Disclose purposes, legal grounds, recipients, international transfer safeguards, retention, rights, security, contact and regulator complaint route. Describe local uninstall/deletion and backup limitations; no false server deletion promise. No mandatory account, so account-deletion rules may not apply; reassess if accounts added. Explain distinct roles of business owner as responsible party for customer records and developer's role for ads/support; legal review required.
## Advertising choices and licence page
Explain provider, online behavior, consent settings, ad identifiers, links to provider privacy resources and no business-record targeting. Include Google-required disclosures and opt-out where applicable; UMP does not itself establish full POPIA compliance. Publish dependency notices and any required attribution; no third-party assets without licences.
## Legal release matrix
- POPIA: data map, lawful processing, minimization, security, retention and cross-border assessment, information officer/rights contact confirmed.
- SARS: full tax invoice fields, VAT status/rate, credit note handling and document retention; accountant review against actual templates.
- Play: privacy policy and Data safety consistent with actual build, third-party SDK and website data; ads and financial declarations accurate.
- Review: counsel/accountant approve final text and template; record policy version and effective date in app and website, verify URLs before launch.
