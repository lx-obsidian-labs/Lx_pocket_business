# 02 — Market research and validation
2026-11-18 | desk research, not field research

## Alternatives
| Alternative | Observed strength | Opportunity / limitation to verify |
|---|---|---|
| Zoho Invoice | Free invoicing, payments, expenses, credit notes, reminders, time tracking and reports | Test complete offline operation, account requirements and export; free price alone is not differentiation. |
| Loyverse POS | Retail point-of-sale and stock workflows | Test offline feature limitations and suitability for service-based quoting; not a direct like-for-like product. |
| Paper/Excel/WhatsApp | Familiar and flexible; often low incremental cost | Repeated entry, inconsistent numbering, reconciliation and backup issues are hypotheses to test. |

Source: https://www.zoho.com/za/invoice/kb/general/zoho-invoice-free.html (verified); https://help.loyverse.com/ (follow-up candidate, specific offline claims not validated here).
## Positioning
One device, no compulsory account, no subscription, local-first records, ZAR and SA tax-document conventions. Differentiate through usable offline quotes-to-cash, dependable export and minimal ads. Avoid saying competitors cannot work offline without reproducible testing.
## Discovery plan
Recruit 5–10 independent operators from at least three sectors. Ask them to show actual workflow with redacted documents: quoting, payment reconciliation, expenses, and monthly handoff. Record task frequency, device type, internet access, existing tool, time, failure points and willingness to view ads. Do not collect real customer details.
Run identical scenarios on competitors and prototype: airplane-mode onboarding, quote, invoice, partial payment, expense, report and export; note sign-in, connection dependencies, storage/restore and any fees. Date/version each test.
## Go/no-go
Proceed to build after evidence of recurring need, at least 4/5 prototype participants completing quote-to-payment unassisted, backup comprehension and acceptable ad placement. Revise scope if inventory, multi-user, tax submission or payment integration prove essential. No market size, demand or revenue numbers established.
